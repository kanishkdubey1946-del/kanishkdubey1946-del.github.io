02MY CONTRIBUTION

Where I came in.

The project explores profile-based scholarship matching, a filtered opportunities feed, school groups and signals for attendance follow-up. Text-first interaction is a focus for limited-data environments.

03KEY DECISIONS

The thinking behind it.

01

Connect information to action

Profiles and filters help turn an opportunity feed into a relevant next step.

02

Design for limited connectivity

Text-first screens and simple language make the intended workflows easier to access.
