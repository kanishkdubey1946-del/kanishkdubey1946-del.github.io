01THE PROBLEM

What needed solving?

Studying often means switching between a chatbot, notes, practice questions, and a planner. ESC brings those activities into a connected flow, so a question leads to useful practice and a plan for what comes next.

02MY CONTRIBUTION

Where I came in.

I worked on the personalised learning experience: combining performance signals with topic-level guidance and relevant resources. The application includes conversational assistance, a specialist studio, and an adaptive learning workspace.

03KEY DECISIONS

The thinking behind it.

01

Keep the conversation central

Chat is the quickest way to ask for help. Structured workspaces provide a separate place to inspect plans, practice, and recorded progress.

02

Connect practice to planning

Assessment results inform topic mastery and the next study plan. Deterministic scoring keeps recorded performance separate from generated explanations.

03

Make sources visible

Uploaded material and web research provide context for specialist responses. Source visibility supports review; it is not a guarantee of accuracy.
